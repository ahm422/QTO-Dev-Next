# Website walkthrough

A page-by-page tour of what visitors see. Web address in brackets.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** Home page, top section

## Top navigation

Every page has the same menu bar: **Home · Products · Services · Projects · Blog · Careers · About**, with a button to get in touch. The footer repeats these plus **FAQs**, social links, and the legal pages.

## Home page  `/`

A stack of sections, all editable from the dashboard:

1. **Hero** — the big headline, sub-text, and main button at the very top.
2. **Services** — a summary of what the company offers.
3. **Statistics** — headline numbers (projects delivered, clients, years, etc.).
4. **How we work / process steps** — the stages of a typical engagement.
5. **Featured work** — a highlight reel of projects or client logos.
6. **Why choose us** — key selling points.
7. **Testimonials** — client quotes.
8. **Industries** — sectors the company serves.
9. **Engagement models** — the ways a client can work with the company (e.g. fixed project, dedicated team), shown like pricing cards.
10. **FAQs** — common questions.
11. **Call to action** — a final prompt to get in touch.

## Products  `/products`

Lists the company's own products (for example an ERP system, "Construct Quest", etc.). Each product has a title, short description, status label ("Live", "In development"…), image, and a list of features. Individual products can have their own detail area.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** Products page

## Services  `/services`  and  `/services/<service-name>`

- The main page lists all services with an icon, short description, and image.
- Clicking one opens its **detail page** with a fuller description, a feature list, the technologies used, and the delivery process.

## Projects / Portfolio  `/projects`  and  `/projects/<project-name>`

- The main page is a filterable grid of past projects (by category).
- Each project detail page is a case study: client, category, the challenge, the solution, results, a technology list, images, and a link to the live site if there is one.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** A project case-study page

## Blog / Insights  `/blogs`  and  `/blogs/<article>`

- A list of articles with a featured/headline article at the top, filterable by category.
- Each article has a cover image, author, estimated read time, tags, and the full text.
- Only articles with "Published" switched on appear here.

## Careers  `/careers`

- Lists open jobs grouped by department, each with location, type (full-time, contract…), description, requirements, and "nice to have" points.
- An **application form** collects the candidate's name, email, phone, cover letter, résumé link, and LinkedIn. Submissions are emailed to the team and stored under **Job Applications** in the dashboard.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** Careers page with a job expanded

## About  `/about`

Company story, mission/values ("work values"), and the team / leadership section with photos, roles, short bios, and LinkedIn links.

## Contact  `/contact`

- A short **contact form**: name, email, subject, message.
- "Other ways to reach us" — phone, email, address, social — all editable in the dashboard.
- On submit, the visitor gets a confirmation email and the team gets a notification email. The message is stored under **Contact Inbox**.

## Get a quote / Requirements  `/requirements`  (also reached from `/get-a-quote`)

A longer, guided questionnaire for serious enquiries: project type, industry, company size, goals, budget range, timeline, preferred contact time, desired features and integrations, success criteria, and a free-text message. Stored under **Requirements** in the dashboard and emailed to the team.

There is also a **client questionnaire** page (`/client-questionnaire`) — a more detailed intake form used for onboarding.

## FAQs  `/faqs`

A standalone list of frequently asked questions and answers (also shown in shorter form on the home page).

## Employee ID card  `/employee/<id>`

A public page showing one employee's photo, name, designation, department, and a QR code. The link is only meaningful if you have it — it is generated by HR from the Employees area. Used as a digital staff ID.

> 📷 **Screenshot:** An employee digital ID card

## Legal pages

**Privacy Policy** `/privacy`, **Terms of Service** `/terms`, **Cookie Policy** `/cookies`. Linked from the footer. These are maintained by a developer, not the dashboard.

## The chat bubble

There is an optional chat widget (bottom-right on the site, and a helper inside the dashboard). It is **switched off by default**. When on, it currently gives canned replies only — it is a placeholder for a future assistant. Ask a developer to enable or connect it.
