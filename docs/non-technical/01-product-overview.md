# Product overview

## What this is

The QTO Dev product is a **website with a built-in control panel**.

- The **website** (`qtodev.com`) is what the public sees: the company's services, products, portfolio, blog, job openings, and contact forms.
- The **admin dashboard** (`qtodev.com/admin`) is a private area where your team changes almost everything on that website — text, images, prices, team photos, blog articles, job posts — without needing a developer.

There is also a small **Employee module** for staff ID: it stores basic employee details and produces a shareable digital ID card with a QR code.

## Who uses it

| Person | What they do | Where |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| Website visitor / potential client | Reads about the company, submits an enquiry or a project brief, applies for a job | The public website |
| Content editor | Updates page text, adds blog posts, uploads images | Admin dashboard |
| Manager / admin | Everything an editor does, plus creating and deleting content | Admin dashboard |
| Super admin | Everything, plus managing which staff have dashboard access, and viewing the activity log | Admin dashboard |
| HR staff | Manages employee records and ID cards | Admin dashboard → Employees |

There is **no public sign-up**. Only people your super admin adds can get into the dashboard.

## What the website does for the business

1. **Explains the offering** — services, products, industries served, the way projects are run, client testimonials, statistics.
2. **Shows proof of work** — a portfolio of projects with case-study detail.
3. **Captures leads** — a contact form and a longer "get a quote" questionnaire. Every submission is emailed to the team and stored in the dashboard.
4. **Recruits** — job listings with an online application form.
5. **Publishes content** — a blog / insights section for SEO and credibility.

## What the admin dashboard lets you change

Almost all visible content is editable:

- Every marketing section on the home page and the Services, Products, Projects, About, Careers, and Contact pages.
- Blog posts (write, edit, schedule, publish).
- Team members, testimonials, client logos / featured work, statistics, FAQs, the technology list, the industries list, engagement/pricing models.
- Contact details and social links.
- The headings, intro text, and calls-to-action at the top of each page.

What you **cannot** change from the dashboard: the page layouts and visual design themselves, and the legal pages' structure (privacy, terms, cookies) — those are developer changes.

## Key ideas to know

- **Published vs. draft.** Most items have a "Published" switch. If it is off, visitors do not see the item; you still do, in the dashboard. Use this to prepare content ahead of time.
- **Order.** Lists (services, team, FAQs…) have a display order you control by dragging or by an order number.
- **Changes go live quickly.** When you save, the public page refreshes itself within moments — usually no wait and nothing else to do.
- **Everything is logged.** Every create, edit, and delete records who did it and when, visible to super admins in the Activity Log.

## The bigger picture (for context)

Behind the scenes the site runs on Cloudflare's platform (a global hosting network), which keeps it fast worldwide and keeps running costs low. Email notifications are sent through Cloudflare's email service. None of this needs your attention day to day — it matters only when accounts are handed over or bills are set up.
